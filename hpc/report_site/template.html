Pac-Man Latent Space Results

Two questions, one pipeline. Do unsupervised sequence embeddings organise gameplay by meaningful behavioural constructs, and what do participants' paths through that space say about how they play?

Research question 1

Do unsupervised latent spaces capture behavioural constructs?

Every model below was trained without labels, reduced to two dimensions with UMAP, and only then compared with Behavlets: hand-coded measures of what the player did in each sequence. The comparison metric is the chance-adjusted neighbourhood hit, the share of a sequence's five nearest latent neighbours that carry the same Behavlet level, with 0 meaning chance. It reads the continuous layout directly and does not depend on HDBSCAN finding discrete clusters, which these spaces mostly do not form.

Three questions, three measures. Local structure: are a sequence's nearest neighbours alike? The neighbourhood hit at five neighbours, in every section up to the profile. Global structure: does that extend to large groups? The same hit as the neighbourhood grows to a thousand sequences, and NMI against HDBSCAN's clusters. Neither sees a label, so together they test whether an unsupervised space makes the constructs salient. Is the information there at all? A ridge regression from the space to each Behavlet's value. It is fitted on the labels, so it says what any representation retains, not what it discovered, and it is kept apart in the last section.

Spatial clusters

Short opening sequences are mostly about where Pac-Man heads first, so these spaces are read through their clusters and the routes they share rather than through Behavlets, which hardly occur that early. Pick a cluster on the map, or from the list, to see its aggregated movement.

Research question 2

How do participants move through the latent space?

Each participant's sequences, in the order they played them, trace a path through the latent space. Three families of measures summarise that path: continuous ones from kernel density estimates, discrete ones from the clusters a participant visits, and recurrence quantification of how often and how persistently they return to the same region. Everything here is computed on each latent space in the controls, so a result that holds in only one of them shows up.
